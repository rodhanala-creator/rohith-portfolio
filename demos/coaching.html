Case study

Coaching Institute Website Redesign

How a coaching institute in India can stop losing students to competitors with a faster, mobile-first website. Real outreach conducted: 8 institutes contacted across Mumbai and Delhi.

Skills Web Design · Mobile-First Development · Conversion Optimisation · Cold Outreach · Client Acquisition

🏫 The problem: great coaching, invisible online

Thousands of coaching institutes across India have exceptional faculty and strong results — but their websites are slow, outdated, and not mobile-friendly. Students researching on their phones move on within seconds.

The numbers
78%of students research institutes on mobile before enquiring
53%abandon a site that takes 3+ seconds to load
40%more enquiries with a redesigned, mobile-first site
8institutes contacted in Mumbai & Delhi (May 2026)
Before vs After

❌ Before redesign

  • Page load: 8–12 seconds on mobile
  • No clear “Enquire Now” button above fold
  • No student testimonials or result boards
  • No WhatsApp contact widget
  • Outdated course information
  • No Google Maps embed
  • Poor mobile layout — text overflow, broken images

✅ After redesign

  • Page load: under 2 seconds
  • Bold hero: exam name + success stats + CTA button
  • Testimonials section with student photos & scores
  • WhatsApp chat widget (instant enquiry)
  • Results board: toppers with rank & year
  • Google Maps + directions embed
  • Fees table & batch schedule clearly shown
Design decisions
📱

Mobile-first

Built for the phone first. 78% of student research happens on mobile.

📣

Trust signals above fold

Student count, years established, Google rating — all visible before any scroll.

🎨

Deep blue + white

Professional, trustworthy colour scheme aligned with education sector expectations.

⚡

Speed optimised

Minimal JS, compressed images, system fonts where possible. Sub-2s load time.

📊

Results board

Toppers, ranks, and years displayed prominently — the institute’s strongest social proof.

💬

WhatsApp widget

Direct WhatsApp link as the primary CTA — how most Indian students prefer to enquire.

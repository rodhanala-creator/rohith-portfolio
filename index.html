Digital Design Case Study

Aura Health

An intelligent mental wellness and habit tracking application focusing on calm, data-driven UI and seamless micro-interactions.

The Challenge

Most mental wellness apps overwhelm users with charts, notifications, and gamification that ultimately induces more anxiety. The goal was to design an interface that feels inherently calming, relying on subtle data visualization and a strictly minimal component library.

The Solution

A dark-mode native, typography-first approach. By limiting the color palette to deep slates and bio-luminescent emeralds, and utilizing soft glassmorphism, Aura guides users gently through their daily check-ins without visual clutter.

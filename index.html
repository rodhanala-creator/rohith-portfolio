Case Study 01 / Healthcare Operations

The Overloaded Dental Front Desk

A local dental practice was staying open but struggling to stay responsive. Enquiries were landing from emails, voice logs, and web forms simultaneously. Staff was heavily overloaded.

The Fix: Designed a calmer visual intake form that instantly parses urgency keywords and filters high-priority patient slots securely down to receptionist alerts.
Result: Zero dropped bookings 15 hours saved / week
Case Study 02 / Local Field Service

The Lost Plumbing Momentum

An active plumbing business was getting customer leads, but losing momentum immediately after the first contact because scheduling required constant back-and-forth emails.

The Fix: Created a simpler pathway right after first contact. Form submissions trigger instant SMS scheduling alerts via Make, allowing instant calendar bookings.
Result: Prompt dispatch responses Automatic routing
Case Study 03 / Beauty & Wellness

The Instant Salon Launch

Built for beauty freelancers and boutique salon owners who need a professional, fast, and gorgeous digital presence without paying thousands of pounds in custom monthly fees.

The Fix: Designed a beautiful salon template optimized for rapid setups, complete with a custom styled WhatsApp booking handshake that converts visitors instantly.
Result: Reusable framework 2-hour custom setup

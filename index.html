Changelog

Release history of a builder

No straight lines here. Every version below shipped something, broke something, or taught me something — usually all three.

v1.0 2016 · Bangalore sunset

Snap Greeks — the spark

First year of computer science, a shared obsession with gadgets, and a tech review blog built with friends. We barely knew HTML — so we learned databases, backends and no-code tools on the fly. The content world moved to social and the blog faded, but the itch to build never did.

First taste of shipping something real.

v2.0 2017–2021 shipped · then closed

Maxi Studio — the design hustle

A college UI/UX workshop fused my Photoshop hobby with engineering, and it became an obsession. I started Marengoo Design Studio, walked away over my own stubbornness, and launched Maxi Studio days later. We reunited the original team and ran it for three relentless years — pitching local stores, landing clients like 101 Reporters, teaching workshops for hundreds of students, even spinning up a post-production studio and a T-shirt brand on the side.

Focus beats doing everything at once. Learned the hard way.

v3.0 2021–2024 · United Kingdom major upgrade

The management pivot

COVID stalled everything, and tools like Canva were rewriting the design industry underneath us. My gap wasn't technical — it was management. So I moved to the UK for an MA in International Business Management, then worked as a Technical Project Manager at an agency, the NHS, and University Hospital Southampton — learning how systems behave at scale. Launched a job consultancy on the side and closed it when it didn't fit the vision.

Knowing how big systems run is a builder's superpower.

v4.0 2025 dark period

The wilderness & the reset

In June 2025 I quit my job. The year that followed was the hardest of the run — stuck, low, unsure of the path. But instead of launching blindly, I went quiet and went deep: AI systems, low-code automation, LLM orchestration. Building the technical foundation for the next chapter, one unglamorous day at a time.

The quiet seasons are where the next version gets built.

v5.0 2026 — present current

Build & ship

Back to my roots with a decade of scars, management experience and a modern AI toolkit. Digi Hyderabadi is live. NaviParka is in active development. The pattern from here is simple: build small, useful products — and narrate the whole thing in the open.

The builder's edge is back.

Case studies

Work presented as stories, not just projects.

A closer look at the challenge, the approach, and why the change mattered.

Case 01 Healthcare workflow

A healthcare-facing workflow that needed calmer intake and clearer routing.

This project started with a team doing its best to stay responsive while requests were arriving through multiple paths. The issue was not effort. The issue was a digital flow that depended too heavily on manual interpretation, inconsistent triage, and too much remembering under pressure.

What the work needed to solve

Incoming requests needed to become easier to capture, easier to prioritise, and easier to pass through the right path without creating extra mental load for the team handling first response.

Challenge

Requests were scattered across different channels, which made urgency harder to spot and consistency harder to maintain.

Approach

The intake journey was mapped, simplified, and reorganised so first contact could enter one clearer flow with better routing logic.

What changed

Routine items became easier to process, higher-priority requests became easier to surface, and the front-end workflow became less reactive.

Result

A calmer first-response process, stronger visibility across incoming requests, and less day-to-day pressure on the people managing the workflow.

Case 02 Lead workflow

A business getting demand, but losing momentum after the first enquiry.

The business was not struggling to generate interest. People were already reaching out. The friction appeared after that first moment, when follow-up relied too heavily on manual effort, scattered inputs, and unclear next steps. The task was to make the digital response path cleaner and more dependable.

What the work needed to solve

The business needed a more structured path from enquiry to response so opportunities would not feel messy, delayed, or overly dependent on someone remembering what to do next.

Challenge

Enquiries were coming in, but the response path was too manual and too easy to slow down when attention moved elsewhere.

Approach

A clearer landing and intake path was shaped so requests had one reliable route into the business, with better visibility and cleaner handling.

What changed

The business gained a simpler way to see new opportunities, respond more consistently, and reduce friction in early follow-up.

Result

Better lead visibility, quicker acknowledgement, and a stronger customer journey from first contact onward.

Case 03 Digital product delivery

A reusable digital product approach built for faster, clearer delivery.

This work focused on creating a more repeatable digital delivery model — one that combined interface thinking, product structure, and implementation logic so new builds could move faster without becoming generic or messy.

What the work needed to solve

The challenge was not only design quality, but repeatability. The goal was to create a digital product approach that could speed up delivery while still leaving room for business-specific adaptation.

Challenge

Starting from scratch every time slows delivery, but rigid outputs can limit usefulness. The balance had to be structure without losing flexibility.

Approach

A reusable product framework was shaped around clear UX structure, implementation logic, and easier customisation for future digital use cases.

What changed

The work became easier to adapt, quicker to deliver, and more consistent in how design and build decisions were handled.

Result

A stronger foundation for repeatable digital product work and a more scalable way to support future client delivery.

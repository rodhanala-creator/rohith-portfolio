ProjectReceptiq AI
RoleAI systems designer · workflow strategist
TypeAI receptionist and enquiry intake system
AudienceService businesses and lean teams

An AI receptionist concept designed to make first-contact workflows faster, clearer, and more consistent.

Receptiq AI was developed as a practical business system for smaller service teams that spend too much time repeating the same conversations. The concept focuses on better enquiry capture, clearer routing, and a more professional digital first response without removing human judgement where it matters.

The problem

Many service businesses deal with repeated questions, inconsistent lead quality, and enquiry threads that arrive with missing context. The challenge is not only speed. It is also structure, clarity, and the quality of the handoff into the business.

  • Enquiries often arrived without the right details.
  • Teams repeated the same qualifying questions again and again.
  • Response quality varied depending on who handled the message first.
  • Routing decisions were slower and less consistent than they should be.

Solution direction

I positioned the product as a structured AI front desk for smaller teams. The system helps capture intent, collect better information, identify the right route, and give staff a cleaner starting point before human follow-up is needed.

What this project shows

AI workflow design, intake UX, automation strategy, operational clarity, and a grounded approach to applying new technology inside a real business context.

ProjectLaunchDesk
RoleDigital project partner · product-minded coordinator
TypeStartup launch and delivery dashboard
AudienceFounders and small product teams

A lean launch dashboard designed to help small teams manage delivery without the weight of a full PM platform.

LaunchDesk was created for startup teams that need visibility, ownership, and momentum, but do not want a heavy project management setup. The concept focuses on the essentials: what is in progress, what is blocked, what needs approval, and what should happen next.

The problem

Early-stage teams often run digital launches through a patchwork of chats, docs, and task boards. That can work for a while, but it creates friction as timelines, dependencies, and approvals begin to overlap.

  • Tasks and launch steps were spread across too many places.
  • Founders lacked one clear view of what needed attention next.
  • Feedback and assets were often disconnected from the work.
  • Project movement felt messy even when the scope was relatively focused.

Solution direction

I designed the project as a calm launch workspace with a clear hierarchy around phases, approvals, tasks, and blocked items. The goal was to create something lighter than a traditional PM system while still giving a small team real operational clarity.

What this project shows

Digital project management thinking, startup-friendly product design, execution structure, and a practical understanding of how lean teams manage launches.

ProjectClientHub
RoleWeb product designer · delivery systems builder
TypeClient portal for freelancers and small agencies
AudienceService-led digital businesses

A client portal concept designed to make freelance and agency delivery feel more premium, organised, and easier to manage.

ClientHub was built around a common service-business problem: projects often run through a mix of email, chat, folders, and scattered updates, even when the underlying work is strong. The concept creates one clean space for onboarding, visibility, approvals, and communication.

The problem

Freelancers and smaller agencies often deliver good work through processes that still feel fragmented to the client. The opportunity was to create a clearer, more premium experience without adding operational weight.

  • Clients had no single place to review progress and files.
  • Onboarding and approvals often happened across multiple tools.
  • Communication could feel less structured than the service itself.
  • The overall delivery experience lacked a stronger sense of polish.

Solution direction

I framed the project as a lightweight service product: a simple portal where clients can see updates, review files, approve work, and stay aligned without unnecessary complexity. The result is a system that improves both the delivery experience and the perceived value of the service.

What this project shows

Client experience design, modern web product thinking, service operations structure, and a stronger link between digital delivery and business perception.

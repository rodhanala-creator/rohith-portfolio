About

Most teams do not need more tools.
They need systems that actually get used.

I've worked across the NHS, startup agencies, and freelance environments, and the same pattern shows up repeatedly: manual work multiplies, communication breaks down, and delivery slows when systems are fragmented.

With a foundation in Computer Science (BE) and Business Management (MA), I combine technical build capability with operational thinking so projects move from idea to implementation without getting stuck between strategy and execution.

My role is usually to simplify the mess: map the workflow, define the system, build the right layer of automation or interface, and make sure the final product supports real business operations rather than becoming another unused tool.

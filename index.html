Aura Patient Intake UX: Systems Architecture & Fluid Design Case Study

How high-end, patient-centric design principles transformed clinical patient onboarding by replacing outdated paper processes with a frictionless digital workspace.

01

Systemic Inefficiencies and Friction

Traditional clinical onboarding operations suffer from massive friction. Patients are presented with dense, non-responsive sheets containing nested options, causing an average of 45% registration drop-off rates. Administrative teams are then forced to manually scan, re-type, and troubleshoot errors.

02

Progressive Disclosure Design System

The design system for Aura reduces cognitive load. By presenting inputs one clear variable block at a time, patients interact with an elegant, responsive UI canvas. We engineered custom micro-indicators, fluid step-by-step transitions, and strict real-time field validation to eliminate validation errors.

ProjectOrchestrix AI
RoleAI Automation Engineer · Product Builder
TypeMulti-channel Operations Platform
FocusAI Systems & Full-stack Automation

Building an AI operations system that qualified leads, routed work, and reduced repetitive admin.

Orchestrix AI was designed as a serious product build rather than a simple chatbot concept. The idea was to create one system that could handle enquiry capture, lead qualification, WhatsApp follow-up, CRM sync, appointment intent, internal notifications, and human review through a single operator dashboard.

Overview

This project was shaped like a full-stack operational product with separate layers for conversational input, classification logic, workflow orchestration, and internal visibility. It positions the work as a real AI automation system that could be published as a GitHub project with frontend, backend, integrations, and database structure.

What this project demonstrates

AI workflow design, architecture thinking, dashboard UX, WhatsApp automation, API integration, database structure, human-in-the-loop review, and product-grade system planning.

The challenge

Service businesses often lose time because enquiries arrive through different channels, qualification is inconsistent, and operators still have to manually copy information into multiple tools. The project addressed that fragmentation by treating intake and routing as a product problem instead of just a messaging problem.

  • Lead data arrived with missing structure and inconsistent quality.
  • WhatsApp, forms, and internal follow-up created disconnected workflows.
  • Manual qualification slowed response speed and created uneven handoffs.
  • Teams lacked a single view of status, urgency, and next action.

Architecture direction

The system was shaped around a modular architecture: a Next.js front-end for operators, server routes for workflow actions, a Supabase layer for structured records, OpenAI-based classification for lead intent, and webhook-based triggers for cross-platform events. This made the project feel more like a real SaaS product than a lightweight automation demo.

ProjectSignalDesk
RoleProduct Engineer · Dashboard Designer
TypeAnalytics SaaS Workspace
FocusAdvanced Dashboard Product

Designing a data-heavy analytics workspace for teams managing leads, performance, and operational visibility.

SignalDesk was framed as a more advanced SaaS-style product for agencies and service operators who need more than spreadsheets and disconnected reports. The platform focused on lead tracking, funnel visibility, client reporting, stage monitoring, and team-level operational insight.

Product direction

The goal was to create a system that could combine analytics clarity with clean product usability. Instead of building a visually noisy dashboard, the interface focused on scannability, trend awareness, drill-down access, and clear separation between summary metrics and operational detail.

What this project demonstrates

Dashboard thinking, data hierarchy, role-based views, chart planning, SaaS UX structure, and front-end engineering for data-dense interfaces.

The challenge

Many reporting products fail because they surface too much at once, flatten priority, and make action harder instead of easier. The challenge here was to build a dashboard system that could support both quick scanning and deeper investigation without overwhelming users.

  • Teams needed both executive summary and operational detail.
  • Lead data, campaign data, and client views had different priorities.
  • Visibility mattered, but density still had to stay usable.
  • Different user roles required different permission layers and views.

Interface system

The interface was organised around KPI modules, trend cards, pipeline tables, account filters, team activity, and reporting panels. A strong information hierarchy made the product feel like a mature SaaS dashboard rather than a collection of widgets.

ProjectFlowForge
RoleSystems Engineer · Workflow Product Designer
TypeInternal Automation Builder
FocusWorkflow Infrastructure Tool

Creating a visual workflow builder for teams that needed real automation without fragile manual processes.

FlowForge was designed as an internal workflow product where users could build automations visually instead of relying on scattered scripts, repeated manual steps, or fragile operational memory. The product focused on triggers, actions, conditions, approvals, delays, and event-driven orchestration.

Why this project stands out

This is a stronger portfolio case study because it is not a simple CRUD app or landing page. It presents a system-level product with visual logic, configuration layers, backend execution, and operational impact — exactly the kind of project that signals advanced development ability.

What this project demonstrates

System design, flow-builder UX, event logic, queue-based thinking, automation architecture, operator control, and advanced internal-tool product direction.

The challenge

Most businesses accumulate repeated tasks long before they build proper workflow systems. The problem is not only inefficiency; it is also inconsistency, missed steps, and low visibility around who did what and what should happen next.

  • Automations were hardcoded or spread across disconnected tools.
  • Workflow changes required technical editing each time.
  • Human approvals were poorly integrated into automated processes.
  • Teams lacked an understandable visual model of system behavior.

Product design

The product was shaped around a visual builder with nodes for triggers, conditions, actions, waits, branches, and approval states. The goal was to make automation design understandable to operators while still connecting to a real execution layer underneath.

ProjectHirePilot
RoleFull-stack Product Builder · AI Workflow Designer

Building an AI-assisted hiring operations platform for screening, tracking, and recruiter workflow visibility.

HirePilot was designed as a richer HR tech project combining recruitment workflow management with AI-assisted screening support. Instead of a simple job board or form-based app, the product was structured as a full recruitment operations platform with candidate stages, role views, recruiter activity, notes, scoring, and interview coordination.

Project scope

The system supported role creation, candidate ingestion, profile review, shortlist management, evaluation notes, stage transitions, and recruiter collaboration. AI was used to assist screening and scoring, but the product still preserved human decision-making and auditability.

What this project demonstrates

Advanced CRUD plus workflow design, AI-assisted product features, dashboard architecture, file and record handling, and strong SaaS product structure.

The challenge

Recruitment products often become hard to use because candidate volume grows quickly, role context gets lost, and collaboration happens across disconnected notes and spreadsheets. The challenge was to build a platform that made candidate flow clear and decision quality easier to maintain.

  • Candidate data needed structure across multiple roles and stages.
  • Recruiters needed visibility without cluttered screens.
  • Scoring assistance had to support, not replace, recruiter judgement.
  • Stage progression and coordination needed stronger internal visibility.

Interface and system logic

The product combined role dashboards, candidate boards, detail drawers, score panels, document-linked metadata, and collaboration notes. This gave the project the feel of a real HR SaaS platform rather than a simple portal.

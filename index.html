03 / The story

A story with
more than one plot.

Ten years. A few reinventions. Quite a few tabs open. Here's how I got here — minus the motivational montage.

Director's note
It was never a straight line.
Good thing.

I started wanting to make things look better. Then I wanted to understand how they worked. Now I want to build the whole thing.

2016
Scene one / The beginning

A tech blog and no clear plan.

In Bangalore, a few friends and I started a technology blog called Snap Greeks. We were curious about gadgets and just curious enough about the internet to try making our own corner of it. We knew very little. We built anyway.

The first lesson: curiosity is a perfectly good starting point.
2016—21
Scene two / The creative years

Pixels turned into a business.

What began with graphic design grew into freelance UX, visual identity work and eventually co-founding Maxc Studio. There were client pitches, ambitious projects, production work, side experiments and plenty of mistakes. For the first time, I wasn't only making designs. I was learning how to run the show.

A good idea needs more than a good-looking screen.
2021—23
Scene three / The pivot

From creating the work to leading it.

I moved into digital project management at Let's Get Coffee. Deadlines, budgets, people, scope and delivery entered the picture. It changed the way I looked at design: not as a final output, but as one part of a much bigger system.

Someone has to connect the dots.
2023—25
Scene four / Bigger systems

A different kind of complexity.

As a Digital Project Manager in the digital department at University Hospital Southampton NHS Foundation Trust, I saw what it takes to coordinate work in a large, complex organisation. That experience strengthened the less glamorous side of building: listening, communicating, planning and delivering responsibly.

The details matter. So do the people.
2025—now
Scene five / Back to building

Full circle. Different toolkit.

I returned to hands-on product building, studying modern software development, experimenting with AI-assisted tools, and learning from the projects that worked — and the ones that didn't. Today I bring design, software and project experience together in freelance work while exploring the next business idea.

No grand finale. Just the next useful thing to make.

Case 01 Healthcare ops

The dental practice that was drowning in admin.

A five-chair dental practice was receiving enquiries across phone, email, walk-ins, and social, but the process behind those enquiries was fragmented. The result was delay, stress, and missed urgency. The solution was a shared intake flow with triage logic, better routing, and automated reminders.

Challenge

Patient enquiries arrived in multiple channels with no single source of truth. Urgent items could easily get buried.

My role

Mapped touchpoints, redesigned intake, and structured the automation logic behind routing and follow-up.

What changed

Every enquiry entered one shared system. Routine requests received faster acknowledgement and urgent items surfaced sooner.

Outcome

Less admin load, quicker first-response handling, and a calmer front-desk workflow.

Case 02 Lead generation

A plumbing business with plenty of leads — and nowhere to put them.

The business had incoming demand from ads, the website, Facebook, and word of mouth, but follow-up was inconsistent. The key issue was not traffic, but lack of structure after the lead arrived. A cleaner landing flow and shared pipeline made the system much easier to run.

Challenge

Lead response depended on manual checking and delayed replies, which weakened conversion.

My role

Audited the intake process, redesigned the landing path, and structured the follow-up flow.

What changed

One landing page, one form, one shared lead board, and faster acknowledgement after enquiry.

Outcome

A simpler system with better visibility, quicker first contact, and stronger conversion potential.

Case 04 Education lead flow

Coaching institute follow-up built for speed and consistency.

Many institutes lose potential students because response handling is slow and scattered. This concept focused on a faster first-reply structure and clearer lead nurturing sequence so interested students had a better path to booking or admission discussion.

Challenge

High enquiry volume but weak manual follow-up meant interest often cooled before action happened.

My role

Researched the market, structured outreach logic, and designed a more reliable response sequence.

What changed

The system moved from scattered replies toward a structured lead flow with clearer next steps.

Outcome

A stronger operating model for institutions that need speed, consistency, and less manual chasing.

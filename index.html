A closer look at the challenge, the approach, and why the change mattered.

Case 01 Healthcare ops

The dental practice that needed a calmer front desk.

This project started with a team doing its best to stay responsive, but patient enquiries were arriving through too many channels at once. The pressure wasn't caused by a lack of effort — it came from a system that asked staff to constantly remember, interpret, and chase too much manually.

What the business was feeling

The front desk was carrying unnecessary mental load. Urgent enquiries could blend into routine ones, and the team needed a process that felt calmer and more reliable day to day.

Challenge

Enquiries were scattered across phone, email, social, and in-person — making it harder to route and prioritise them consistently.

Approach

The intake journey was mapped, simplified, and reorganised so all first contact entered one clearer shared flow.

What changed

Routine requests became easier to process, urgent cases became easier to surface, and the team had less manual checking to do.

Result

A calmer first-response process, better visibility across all incoming contact, and less daily pressure on the team.

Case 02 Lead generation

The plumbing business getting interest — but losing rhythm after the enquiry.

The business wasn't struggling to be found. People were reaching out. The real problem started after that moment — when follow-up relied too much on memory, manual checking, and fragmented communication. The task was to remove the drag between "someone is interested" and "someone receives a clear response."

What the business was feeling

Enough demand to stay busy, but not enough structure to handle every lead with the same speed. That inconsistency made the whole process heavier than it needed to be.

Challenge

Leads were coming in, but the follow-up path depended too heavily on manual effort and often slowed down unnecessarily.

Approach

A cleaner landing path and shared lead structure were built so enquiries had one clear route into the business.

What changed

The business gained a simpler way to see new opportunities, respond faster, and reduce the friction in early follow-up.

Result

Better lead visibility, quicker acknowledgement, and stronger consistency throughout the early customer journey.

Case 03 Productized template

The salon template built to make launching feel less intimidating.

Many smaller salons rely heavily on Instagram DMs, informal enquiry handling, or a digital presence that doesn't reflect the quality of the service they actually provide. This project was designed to give salon owners a polished, professional starting point — without asking them to begin from scratch or wait months for a bespoke build.

What the business was feeling

Launching online felt expensive, confusing, and too dependent on finding the right developer at the right time. The template was designed to reduce that pressure and create a faster, clearer path to being visible and bookable online.

Challenge

Small salons need a professional website, but not the cost, delay, or complexity of a full bespoke build from scratch.

Approach

A reusable, well-designed template was built to balance launch speed, visual quality, and flexibility for later customisation.

What changed

Salon owners gained a faster path to launch. The template also became a productized asset that supports ongoing client work.

Result

A more accessible digital starting point for salons and a stronger, repeatable service offer on the freelance side.

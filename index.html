Case Study 01 // Clinical Operations & Booking

Aura: Elegant Intake UX Linked to AI Emergency Triage

How we completely transformed a busy multi-chair private dental clinic's messy, manual phone-booking bottleneck into a beautiful, step-by-step patient registration portal linked to an intelligent AI emergency classifier.

1. Project Niche Service-Based Medical/Clinical
2. My Role Product Design & Automations
3. Tools Implemented Make.com, Airtable, OpenAI API, Twilio
4. Quantifiable ROI 15 Admin Hours Saved / Week

The Operational Friction (Before)

  • ❌ Patients forced onto long phone hold queues just to check slot availability.
  • ❌ Front desk staff spend 15+ hours/week re-keying registration details from paper sheets.
  • ❌ Critical emergencies (throbbing pain/broken crown) waiting hours for sorting, losing high-value clients.

The Quieter System (After)

  • ✓ Gorgeous, step-by-step mobile-responsive digital intake workflow.
  • ✓ Automated Airtable ledger sorting instantly updates and organizes client data.
  • ✓ AI Emergency Triage system alerts on-call clinical teams via Twilio SMS in under 90 seconds.

The Challenge & Bottleneck

A busy multi-chair private dental practice suffered from immense administrative strain. Patient registrations, appointment scheduling, and emergency triage calls were handled entirely over the phone by front-desk staff. With dozens of incoming calls daily, staff spent over 15 hours every week manually tracking information, checking calendars, and keying customer details into databases.

More critically, patients experiencing extreme emergency conditions—like trauma, bleeding, or throbbing root canal pain—often had to wait on hold behind standard cleaning inquiries. The business was losing high-value urgent patients due to phone delays, while the staff felt constantly burned out by the manual overhead.

The Blueprint Strategy

We completely bypassed the manual bottleneck by building a two-part digital system:

  1. Premium Front-End Intake Portal: A gorgeous, step-by-step digital form that takes patients through medical demographics, symptom tags, and appointment availability. This feels incredibly premium and works flawlessly on mobile.
  2. AI Triage Engine (Make.com + OpenAI): On submission, the data is instantly processed. If symptoms indicate emergency conditions, an AI routing webhook triggers a priority alarm. It drops the patient at the top of an Airtable dispatch ledger, and alerts on-duty clinical staff via Twilio SMS for immediate callback. Standard cleaning requests are systematically matched directly into calendar booking slots.

This combination ensured urgent patients received clinical callbacks within 90 seconds, and eliminated manual registration re-keying completely.

The Automation Schema
1. Intake UI Collection
• User inputs demographics & symptoms.
• Frontend validations prevent blank submissions.
2. AI Router Webhook
• Prompt classifies symptoms into priority values.
• If URGENT: Fired Twilio on-call alarm.
• If ROUTINE: Automatically scheduled in booking slot.

Case Study 02 // B2B Startup Onboarding

ClientBridge: Auto-Onboarding & File Verification Portal

How we designed an automated, highly secure client document vault and portal for a premium startup accelerator, eliminating manual spreadsheet chasing and milestone tracking completely.

1. Project Niche B2B Startups & Agencies
2. My Role UX Design & Portal Automations
3. Tools Implemented React-Tailwind, Node, Make.com, Airtable SDK
4. Quantifiable ROI 12 Onboarding Hours Saved / Week

The Operational Friction (Before)

  • ❌ Founders send critical onboarding files across scattered Slack uploads, GDrive, and emails.
  • ❌ Managers spend 12+ hours/week manually verifying document formats and updating tracking spreadsheets.
  • ❌ No real-time tracking visibility results in endless back-and-forth status check emails.

The Quieter System (After)

  • ✓ Single-token handshake workspace grants instant access to a secure document vault.
  • ✓ Make.com automations parse and check uploaded files automatically, updating CRM database lists instantly.
  • ✓ Founders get clear visual milestone lists showing real-time onboarding checklist completion.

The Challenge & Bottleneck

An active startup consulting accelerator was struggling to onboard new founders efficiently. Essential documents—incorporation certificates, cap tables, founders' identity files, and business plans—were completely scattered. Founders sent documents over endless email threads, Slack uploads, and Google Drive links.

The accelerator's managers spent over 12 hours a week manually downloading, organizing, and checking off document requirements. Founders had no visual clarity on where they stood in the onboarding queue, leading to high friction, frustration, and constant "where do things stand?" check-in emails.

The Blueprint Strategy

We solved this bottleneck by designing and automating **ClientBridge**:

  1. Zero-Registration Login UX: Instead of forcing founders to memorize another login, they receive a secure, cryptographically generated workspace token (`ACME-2026`) that logs them directly into their beautiful portal instantly.
  2. Smart File Uploader (Make + Airtable Integration): When a user uploads their balance sheets or Cap Tables into the custom dashboard, a backend automation checks the file type, updates the status checkmark in Airtable, and recalculates the progress completion percentage in real-time.

This gave founders total clarity over their onboarding checklist and reduced manual data sorting to absolute zero.

The Automation Schema
1. Token Check Handshake
• User requests entry using workspace code.
• Access token verified against CRM.
2. Asset Verification Router
• File gets uploaded to folder structures.
• Milestones status update dynamically inside CRM.

Case Study 03 // Trade & Home Services Ops

FlowPilot: Beautiful Booking Matrix with Auto-SMS Dispatches

How we designed a gorgeous trade-booking application for a local heating and plumbing trade business, replacing paper appointment cards with a real-time drag-and-drop dispatcher matrix.

1. Project Niche Home Services & Trade Brands
2. My Role UI/UX Design & Make.com Integration
3. Tools Implemented Tailwind Grid, Make.com, Airtable, Twilio API
4. Quantifiable ROI -80% Operational Phone calls

The Operational Friction (Before)

  • ❌ Job schedules tracked manually across office whiteboards and paper calendars.
  • ❌ Coordinators spend hours daily calling field engineers and clients to coordinate schedules.
  • ❌ No automatic dispatch confirmations result in missed slots and delayed invoice processing.

The Quieter System (After)

  • ✓ Drag-and-drop Kanban dispatch board replaces whiteboard scheduling clutter.
  • ✓ Webhook triggers instantly text customers tracking links and scheduled arrival times.
  • ✓ Synchronized Airtable records create draft invoices automatically upon job completion.

The Challenge & Bottleneck

An active regional plumbing, heating, and air conditioning maintenance business was struggling with scheduling and coordinator bottlenecks. Inquiries came from a messy website contact form, and jobs were managed manually on whiteboards and shared paper agendas.

Dispatches were slow: engineers frequently drove to the wrong locations, customers were left waiting without updates, and billing was delayed because paperwork went missing. This lack of organization impacted customer satisfaction and caused billing leaks.

The Blueprint Strategy

We completely automated this system by designing **FlowPilot**:

  1. Interactive Field Matrix Board: A visual, drag-and-drop kanban dispatcher dashboard that gives coordinators instant visual control over job pipelines.
  2. State-Driven Text Triggers (Airtable + Twilio Webhooks): Moving a job card to "Scheduled" triggers a webhook. The automation pulls the assigned engineer's schedule, texts the customer a tracking link with estimated arrival times, and drafts a digital invoice draft in the system automatically.

This streamlined communication, reduced scheduling overhead, and eliminated the need for manual follow-up calls.

The Automation Schema
1. Drag Dispatch Triggers
• Coordinator drags card to target column stage.
• DOM event captures the new status.
2. SMS & Calendar Pipelines
• Automatically texts customer with tracker.
• Pre-populates the engineer's calendar events.

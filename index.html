Case 01 Healthcare ops

The dental practice that needed a calmer front desk.

This project started with a team that was doing its best to stay responsive, but patient enquiries were arriving through too many channels at once. The pressure was not caused by lack of effort. It came from a system that asked staff to constantly remember, interpret, and chase too much manually.

What the business was feeling

The front desk was carrying unnecessary mental load. Urgent enquiries could blend in with routine ones, and the team needed a process that felt calmer and more reliable day to day.

Challenge

Enquiries were scattered across phone, email, social, and in-person requests, making it harder to route and prioritise them well.

Approach

The intake journey was mapped, simplified, and reorganised so all first contact could enter one clearer shared flow.

What changed

Routine requests became easier to process, urgent cases became easier to surface, and the team had less manual checking to do.

Result

A calmer first-response process, better visibility, and less pressure on the people handling patient communication.

Case 02 Lead generation

The plumbing business that was getting interest, but losing rhythm after the enquiry.

The business was not struggling to get noticed. People were reaching out. The real problem began after that moment, when follow-up relied too much on manual checking, memory, and fragmented communication. The task was to remove the drag between “someone is interested” and “someone receives a clear response.”

What the business was feeling

The team had enough demand to stay busy, but not enough structure to handle every lead with the same speed and consistency. That uncertainty made the process heavier than it needed to be.

Challenge

Leads were arriving, but the follow-up path was too dependent on manual effort and could slow down unnecessarily.

Approach

A cleaner landing path and shared lead structure were designed so enquiries had one clearer route into the business.

What changed

The business gained a simpler way to see new opportunities, respond faster, and reduce follow-up friction.

Result

Better visibility, quicker acknowledgement, and stronger consistency in the early stage of the customer journey.

Case 03 Productized template

The salon template built to make launching feel less intimidating.

Many smaller salons still rely heavily on Instagram messages, informal enquiry handling, or a digital setup that does not reflect the quality of the service they actually provide. This project was designed to give salon owners a polished starting point without asking them to begin from scratch, while also creating a more repeatable offer on the service side.

What the business was feeling

Launching online can feel expensive, confusing, and too dependent on custom work. This template was designed to reduce that pressure and create a faster, clearer route to being visible online.

Challenge

Small salons often need a professional website, but not the cost, delay, or complexity of a full bespoke build.

Approach

A reusable template was designed to balance speed, polish, and enough flexibility for customisation later.

What changed

Salon owners gained a faster path to launch, while the template also became a product that supports future client work.

Result

A more accessible digital starting point for salons and a stronger productized service asset for the portfolio.

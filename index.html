The Unpolished Journey

I build. I fail.
I learn. I ship.

I am a Digital & AI Consultant. But before the frameworks and the AI orchestrations, there were broken partnerships, failed startups, and a lot of relentless hustle. Here is how I got here.

2016 — Bangalore

The Spark & Snap Greeks

My story starts in my first year of computer science engineering. I've always had an entrepreneurial itch. Fueled by a passion for digital gadgets, I teamed up with friends to launch Snap Greeks, a tech review blog. We were young, didn't know what a business was, and barely knew HTML. But we learned databases, backend systems, and no-code tools on the fly. It eventually fizzled out as the content world shifted toward social media and video, but it gave me my first taste of building and shipping.

2017 - 2021

The Design Hustle: Maxc Studio

A college workshop introduced me to UI/UX, bridging my old hobby of Photoshop with computer science. It became an obsession. I was analyzing the UX of every app I touched. I started Marengoo Design Studio, walked away due to my own stubbornness, and days later launched Maxc Studio.

We eventually reunited the original team and ran Maxi for three relentless years. We pitched local brick-and-mortar stores, secured major clients like 101 Reporters and Food Garden, hosted workshops for hundreds of students, and even launched side-brands like a post-production studio (Twin Monkeys) and a T-shirt company. We learned the hard way about the power of focus versus the chaos of trying everything at once.

2021 - 2024 — United Kingdom

The Pivot to Management

When COVID hit, everything stalled. We tried launching an ed-tech platform (Brain Eaters), which failed. Simultaneously, I noticed the design industry shifting. Tools like Canva were democratizing design, and I foresaw an automated future for UI/UX. I realized my true gap wasn't technical—it was management.

I made the hard call to leave Maxi Studio and moved to the UK to pursue an MA in International Business Management. This opened the doors to corporate roles. I worked as a Technical Project Manager at an agency, the NHS, and University Southampton Hospital, learning how systems operate at scale. I also launched a job consultancy, What The Job, which I quickly closed when it didn't align with my vision.

2025

The Wilderness & The Reset

In June 2025, I quit my job. What followed was a year of profound struggle. I felt stuck, depressed, and confused about my path. I questioned why I ever left UI/UX. But this wilderness period forced me to pause and adapt. Instead of launching blindly, I spent this time deep-diving into AI, low-code automations, and LLM orchestration systems. I was quietly building the technical foundation for the next chapter of my life.

2026 — Present

The Rebirth: Build & Ship

I am finally back to my roots, but this time with a decade of harsh lessons, management experience, and cutting-edge technical skills. I've broken out of the paralysis of perfection. Today, I operate as a Digital & AI Consultant, designing bespoke digital tools and deploying multi-agent systems for businesses.

And to prove I've got my builder's edge back? I just launched Digi Hyderabadi.

Case studies

A closer look at the challenge, the approach, and why the change mattered.

Case 01 Healthcare workflow

The healthcare workflow that needed a calmer intake process.

This project started with a team doing its best to stay responsive, but incoming requests were arriving through too many channels at once. The pressure was not caused by a lack of effort — it came from a system that asked staff to remember, interpret, and chase too much manually.

What the team was feeling

First-response work carried unnecessary mental load. Different types of requests could blend together, and the team needed a clearer process that felt more reliable day to day.

Challenge

Requests were scattered across multiple channels, making it harder to route and prioritise them consistently.

Approach

The intake journey was mapped, simplified, and reorganised so first contact entered one clearer shared flow.

What changed

Routine requests became easier to process, urgent issues became easier to surface, and the team had less manual checking to do.

Result

A calmer first-response process, better visibility across incoming contact, and less daily pressure on the team.

Case 02 Lead workflow

The business losing rhythm after first enquiry.

The business was not struggling to be found. People were reaching out. The real problem started after that moment — when follow-up relied too much on memory, manual checking, and fragmented communication. The task was to remove the drag between interest and clear response.

What the business was feeling

There was enough demand to stay busy, but not enough structure to handle every lead with the same speed. That inconsistency made the whole process heavier than it needed to be.

Challenge

Leads were coming in, but the follow-up path depended too heavily on manual effort and often slowed down unnecessarily.

Approach

A cleaner landing path and shared lead structure were built so enquiries had one clear route into the business.

What changed

The business gained a simpler way to see new opportunities, respond faster, and reduce friction in early follow-up.

Result

Better lead visibility, quicker acknowledgement, and stronger consistency throughout the early customer journey.

Case 03 Digital product delivery

A reusable digital product approach built for faster, clearer delivery.

This work focused on creating a more repeatable digital delivery model — one that combined interface thinking, product structure, and implementation logic so new builds could move faster without becoming generic or messy.

What the work needed to solve

The challenge was not only design quality, but repeatability. The goal was to create a digital product approach that could speed up delivery while still leaving room for business-specific adaptation.

Challenge

Starting from scratch every time slows delivery, but rigid outputs can limit usefulness. The balance had to be structure without losing flexibility.

Approach

A reusable product framework was shaped around clear UX structure, implementation logic, and easier customisation for future digital use cases.

What changed

The work became easier to adapt, quicker to deliver, and more consistent in how design and build decisions were handled.

Result

A stronger foundation for repeatable digital product work and a more scalable way to support future client delivery.
